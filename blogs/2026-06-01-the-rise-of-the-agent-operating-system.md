The chatbot was the GUI of the AI era. The agent operating system is the kernel underneath it. From the AIOS research paper to Hermes, Claude, and OpenAI, a new stack is forming with the same parts every OS has had for fifty years: a scheduler, a memory layer, a filesystem, drivers, and a protocol for talking to the outside world. Here is what the agent OS looks like and why it matters.
